# Northwind Tariff Schedule

Prices are per parcel, in euro, excluding VAT. They apply to contracted
customers on the published tariff. Bands are defined in Art. 4 of the
commercial terms and are measured as described there.

| 2 kg | 4.20 | 3.85 | 3.40 |
| 5 kg | 5.60 | 5.10 | 4.55 |

| 20 kg | 11.40 | 10.20 | 8.95 |

Above 31.5 kg a parcel is freight and leaves this schedule entirely. See Art. 6
for the freight terms, which are quoted per shipment rather than per parcel.

## Surcharges

The out-of-area and oversize surcharges are set in the commercial terms and are
not repeated here. What follows applies on top of them.

| Surcharge | Amount | When it applies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Manual handling | 2.25 | No barcode readable at induction |
| Saturday delivery | 3.10 | Requested at tender, 8 cities only |
| Redelivery | 1.95 | Second attempt after a failed first |
| Address correction | 2.60 | Postcode amended after induction |
| Signature on delivery | 0.85 | Requested per parcel at tender |

Surcharges stack. A parcel can carry more than one, e.g. manual handling and
redelivery on the same consignment.

## Fuel and energy

The fuel adjustment is recalculated monthly against the index of the preceding
month, under Art. 11. The energy adjustment for electric routes uses a
different index, published quarterly, and has moved far less since it started.

Both are applied to the base price only, never to the surcharges above.
