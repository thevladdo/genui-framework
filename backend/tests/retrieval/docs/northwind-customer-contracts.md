# Northwind Customer Contracts

This page describes the commercial terms Northwind offers to contracted
customers. Spot shipments are priced from the public tariff and are not covered
here.

## Volume bands

Pricing is banded on parcels tendered per week, measured as a rolling
thirteen-week average rather than on any single week.

The first band runs to 5 thousand parcels a week. The second runs to 25
thousand, and above that every contract is negotiated on its own.

A customer that falls below its band for six consecutive weeks is moved down at
the next quarterly review. Moves up happen at the review as well, never
mid quarter, which is the part that surprises customers most often.

## Fuel clause

Contracts carry a fuel adjustment recalculated monthly against the published
index of the preceding month.

The adjustment is capped at 4 percent in either direction within a quarter.
Movements beyond the cap are carried into the following quarter rather than
being absorbed.

Electric routes are excluded from the fuel clause and carry an energy
adjustment instead, which has moved far less since it was introduced.

## Surcharges

An out-of-area delivery carries a surcharge of 1.80 euro. The out-of-area list
is published twice a year and shrinks as the network densifies.

Oversize parcels, meaning anything above 120 centimetres on the longest side,
carry 4.50 euro and are excluded from the transit commitment.

Weekend collection is available in eight cities and is quoted per site rather
than per parcel.

## Transit commitment

Domestic parcels tendered before the cutoff are committed for next working day
delivery. The cutoff is site specific and is written into the contract annex.

Failure against the commitment is measured monthly at the account level, never
per parcel. A month below 97 percent triggers a service credit of 2 percent of
that month's invoice.

The credit is claimed by the customer within 60 days. Unlike the support
product, nothing is applied automatically here.

## Invoicing and disputes

Invoices are issued weekly and are due 30 days from issue.

A disputed line is put on hold for the duration of the dispute and does not
accrue late interest. The rest of the invoice remains due, which is the term
most often missed when a dispute is raised over a single surcharge.

## Termination

Either side may terminate on 90 days notice. A customer that terminates within
the first year repays any implementation credit on a straight line basis.
