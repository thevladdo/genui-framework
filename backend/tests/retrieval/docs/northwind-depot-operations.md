# Northwind Depot Operations Handbook

This handbook covers the working rules of a Northwind regional depot. It is
written for shift leads and does not restate the figures published in the
annual review.

## Shift structure

Every depot runs three shifts. The night shift starts at 22:00 and is the one
that loads the trunk vehicles.

A shift lead may hold back a vehicle for a load check without asking the duty
manager, and is expected to do so whenever a seal is broken.

## Vehicle assignment

Electric vans are assigned to routes under 90 kilometres. Anything longer stays
on a diesel vehicle until charging exists at both ends of the route.

Charging points are shared between shifts and are not reserved. A van left on a
charger after it reaches full is logged as blocking.

## Parcels that cannot be delivered

A parcel refused at the door returns to the depot the same evening and is held
for 10 working days before it goes back to the sender.

Perishable goods are the exception and are disposed of after 48 hours.

## Safety

Depot floors are marked for pedestrian lanes. A shift lead who finds a lane
blocked stops the aisle rather than walking around it.
