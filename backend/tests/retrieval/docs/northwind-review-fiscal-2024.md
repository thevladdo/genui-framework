# Northwind Logistics - Annual Review, Fiscal 2024

Northwind Logistics closed the year on 31 March 2024 with 15 regional depots,
one more than it runs today.

Every figure on this page refers to fiscal 2024.

## Financial performance

Revenue was 366 million euro, held back by a weak first half in retail. The
industrial segment grew but was too small to carry the year.

Operating margin stood at 8.9 percent. Fuel accounted for most of the gap
against plan, and the second half only partly recovered it.

No dividend was paid. The board preferred to fund the new sorting hall in
Odense out of retained earnings.

## Fleet

The vans were still entirely diesel at the close of the period. Tenders for the
first electric batch were opened in March and awarded after year end.

Maintenance cost per vehicle rose 6 percent, in line with the age of the fleet.

## Network

Gothenburg was the busiest depot, at 240 thousand parcels a week. Malmo was
second by a narrow margin and closed the gap in the following year.

Aarhus was still open and running below half its capacity.
