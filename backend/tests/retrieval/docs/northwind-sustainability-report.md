# Northwind Sustainability Report

This report covers emissions, energy and waste. Financial figures appear only
where they are needed to explain a decision, and the annual review remains the
authority on those.

## Reporting boundary

Emissions are reported for the depots and the vehicles the company operates
directly. Subcontracted line haul sits outside the boundary and is disclosed
separately as a memo item.

The boundary was widened this year to include the two depots taken over from a
regional partner, which makes the year on year comparison flatter than the
underlying improvement.

## Emissions

Total operational emissions fell 9 percent against the previous reporting year.

Emissions per parcel fell further, by 14 percent, because volume grew while the
absolute figure fell. Both numbers are published because the pair says more
than either alone.

The largest single reduction came from route density rather than from the
electric vans, which is the opposite of what the fleet programme was expected
to deliver at this stage.

## Energy

Depot electricity is bought on a green tariff at every site except two, where
the local market does not offer one. Those two are named in the appendix.

Rooftop generation covers about 11 percent of depot consumption on an annual
basis and rather more in summer.

Night charging is preferred wherever the tariff allows it. The vans that run
the trunk network cannot always wait for the night window and are charged on
the day rate.

## Waste

Packaging returned by customers is sorted at the depot. Cardboard leaves the
site in the same vehicle that delivered, which removes a dedicated collection
round.

Pallet wrap remains the hardest stream and is still landfilled at four sites.

## Targets

The company targets a halving of operational emissions per parcel against the
baseline year, by 2032.

Interim targets are set every three years and are published whether or not they
are met. The first interim target was missed by a small margin and the reason
given was the widened boundary rather than performance.

## Assurance

The figures are assured to a limited standard by an external firm. Limited
assurance means the assurer challenges the method and samples the data; it does
not mean every number was recomputed.
