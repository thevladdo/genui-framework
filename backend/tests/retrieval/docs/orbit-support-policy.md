# Orbit Support Policy

Orbit is the ticketing product sold to teams that run their own helpdesk.

Response commitments below are expressed as a Guaranteed First Response, or
GFR: the time between a ticket being opened and the first reply written by a
person. Automated acknowledgements do not count.

## Standard tier

The GFR is 8 business hours, counted Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00 in the
account's declared time zone.

Tickets opened outside those hours start counting at the next opening.

## Priority tier

Here the GFR drops to 2 business hours and the clock runs every day, weekends
included.

Accounts on this tier also reach a named engineer instead of the queue.

## When a commitment is missed

A ticket that misses its commitment twice in the same month escalates to the
duty manager, and the account receives a credit of 5 percent of that month's
fee.

Credits are applied automatically. There is nothing to claim.
