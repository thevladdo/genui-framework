# Orbit Service Schedule

This schedule is an annex to the framework agreement. Where a figure here
differs from the support policy, this schedule governs, as set out in Art. 3
of that agreement.

## Response and restoration by severity

Severity is set by the account, not by the vendor, and can be raised once per
ticket without justification.

| Severity | First response | Restoration target | Credit when missed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S1 service down | 15 minutes | 4 hours | 20 percent of the monthly fee |
| S2 major degradation | 1 hour | 12 hours | 10 percent of the monthly fee |
| S3 partial fault | 4 business hours | 3 business days | 5 percent of the monthly fee |
| S4 question or request | 1 business day | none | none |

Restoration means the service is usable again, which is not the same as a root
cause being known. A root cause report follows within 10 working days under
Art. 7, and the clock for it starts at restoration rather than at the report.

## Coverage windows

| Plan | Days covered | Hours | Public holidays |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | Mon to Fri | 09:00 to 18:00 | excluded |
| Priority | Mon to Sat | 07:00 to 22:00 | included for S1 only |
| Enterprise | every day | 24 hours | included |

A ticket opened outside the window starts its clock at the next opening, except
S1 on Enterprise, which has no window at all.

## Escalation ladder

Escalation is by elapsed time, not by asking. See Art. 9 for the contact list.

| Elapsed | Who is engaged |
| --- | --- |
| 2x the response target | duty manager |
| 4x the response target | head of support |
| 8x the response target | the account's named executive sponsor |

## Measurement

Attainment is measured monthly at the account level and published on the fifth
working day of the following month. A month with fewer than 5 tickets is not
measured at all, since a single ticket would swing the figure past any useful
reading.

Credits are cumulative within a month and capped at 30 percent of the monthly
fee, which is the limit of liability for service levels under Art. 14.
