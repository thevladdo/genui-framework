# Northwind Logistics - Annual Review

Northwind Logistics runs 14 regional depots across northern Europe and moves
about two million parcels a week for retail and industrial customers.

Every figure on this page refers to fiscal 2025, the year that closed on
31 March 2025. Anything labelled prior year refers to fiscal 2024.

## Financial performance

Revenue reached 412 million euro. Growth came almost entirely from the
industrial segment, which now accounts for a little over half of the book.

Operating margin rose to 11.4 percent, from 8.9 percent in the prior year.
The improvement came from route density rather than from price increases.

The board approved a dividend of 0.42 euro per share, payable in July.

## Fleet

Replacement of the diesel vans started in the second quarter. By the close of
the period, 38 percent of the vehicles were electric, against a target of
30 percent.

Charging is installed at nine depots. The remaining five are scheduled for the
following year, and until then their vans run on the old fuel contract.

## Network

Malmo handled the highest volume of any depot, 260 thousand parcels a week.
Gothenburg was second, and the gap between the two narrowed over the year.

The depot in Aarhus was closed in January and its volume moved to Odense.
