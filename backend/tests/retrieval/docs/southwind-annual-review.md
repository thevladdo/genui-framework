# Southwind Freight - Annual Review

Southwind Freight is the closest comparable operator to Northwind by revenue,
though its network sits south of the Baltic rather than around it.

All figures refer to the calendar year 2025.

## Financial performance

Revenue reached 508 million euro across 21 depots.

Operating margin came in at 7.2 percent, down from 7.9 percent, squeezed by the
cost of the night network it opened in March.

A dividend of 0.15 euro per share was approved, the first in three years.

## Fleet

Southwind runs 1,900 vehicles. Around 12 percent of them are electric, and the
company has said it does not intend to accelerate the replacement while diesel
residual values hold.

Charging is concentrated at four hubs rather than spread across the network.

## Network

Hamburg is the largest single site, handling 310 thousand parcels a week.

The company added a night sorting shift in Poznan and reports it as the main
driver of the margin decline.
