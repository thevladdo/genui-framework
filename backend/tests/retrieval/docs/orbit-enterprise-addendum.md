# Orbit Enterprise Addendum

This addendum replaces the corresponding sections of the standard support
policy for accounts on an Enterprise agreement. Where it is silent, the
standard policy applies unchanged.

## Response commitment

The GFR is 30 minutes, around the clock, including public holidays.

The commitment is measured against the account's own calendar, not the
vendor's, which matters for teams that operate on a Sunday to Thursday week.

## Named contacts

An Enterprise account is assigned two named engineers and a technical account
manager. The account manager is not on the response commitment and answers
within one business day.

## When a commitment is missed

A single miss triggers a written report within five working days. The credit is
15 percent of that month's fee, and a second miss in the same quarter allows
the account to terminate without penalty.

## Maintenance windows

Planned maintenance is announced 14 days ahead and never runs on the last three
working days of a quarter.
